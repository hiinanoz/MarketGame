Start with a tiny corner shop and build a business empire!

Run your own store in a cozy 3D town. Move with a simple one-finger joystick, unload the delivery truck, carry boxes to the shelves, take payments at the checkout and collect your earnings. Every coin goes back into your shop: new shelves, bigger storage, more checkouts and a whole new hall.

GROW YOUR SHOP
• Order from the wholesaler and watch the truck deliver to your back storage
• Sell fresh vegetables, bread, meat, sauces and ready meals
• Hire cashiers, carriers, cleaners, security and a store manager
• Keep customers happy: clean floors, short queues, full shelves
• Run campaigns, sale days and online orders

BUILD AN EMPIRE
• Open suppliers, factories, a logistics company and power plants
• Build houses, rent them out and grow the town
• Take loans, open your own bank, trade on the stock market
• Win the election and become the mayor
• 100 levels of goals, daily quests and login rewards

YOUR WAY
• Customize your character
• Decorate your shop
• Earn while you are away
• Calm music that follows your day, from morning rush to night shift
• Game Center achievements and leaderboards
• Your progress syncs with iCloud across iPhone and iPad

No account needed and no personal data collected.
